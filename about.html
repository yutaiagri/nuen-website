About NUEN

Company Profile

Shandong NUEN Automotive Technology Co., Ltd.

NUEN is a Sino-foreign joint venture integrating R&D, production and service — a leading expert in low-speed electric vehicles (LSEV): electric city cars, electric pickup trucks, autonomous driving vehicles and special-purpose four-wheel EVs.

As one of the very few Chinese manufacturers holding EEC whole-vehicle type approval & COC certificates, NUEN is the first-choice partner for distributors, leasing companies and government projects across Europe and the Middle East.

  • First-mover on vehicle OBD management systems in low-speed EVs
  • Fuel-to-EV, high/low speed and 4WD conversion expertise
  • 66 right-hand-drive product variants (79+ models incl. series)
  • Remote technical support with real-time ECU data collection
