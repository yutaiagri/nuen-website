FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything buyers ask before placing an order.

Are NUEN electric vehicles EEC and COC certified?

Yes. NUEN's main export models carry full EEC whole-vehicle type approval and COC certificates, ensuring smooth customs clearance and legal registration in Europe.

What is the minimum order quantity (MOQ)?

Most NUEN models have an MOQ of 1 unit — ideal for distributors and dealers who want to test the market first. Bulk and fleet pricing is available.

Can NUEN customize vehicles for my market?

Yes. We support OEM/ODM deep customization: RHD or LHD, lithium or lead-acid batteries, 100-300 km range, wheelbase and cargo volume, 4WD, fuel-to-EV conversions and extreme climate packages.

What is the delivery lead time?

Typical lead time is 15-30 days depending on configuration and order size. We export from Qingdao Port with CIF or FOB terms.

Do you ship to my country?

NUEN exports to 138 countries including USA, Mexico, UK, UAE and Japan. We also assist with destination compliance such as DOT, UL, UKCA, PSE and RoHS.
